Style notes extracted:
- Layered street clothes, practical not stylish: warm jacket, beanie/cap, sturdy shoes
- The load is the iconography: bags and carts full of green glass bottles
- Dignity framing: resourceful, fast, knows every Späti and every bin route
- Game framing: the player is the hero of their own route — never the butt of a joke

Art direction decisions:
- Character: mustard beanie, olive puffer, canvas backpack with bottles, worn jeans
- Palette: Berlin night amber (streetlights) + bottle green + concrete blue-grey
- Props via Modly: bottle, crate, Späti kiosk, BVG ticket machine
